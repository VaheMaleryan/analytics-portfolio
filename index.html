01VoiceAI: from product metrics to billing controlsSynthetic scenario · July–September 2026+

Business question

Can product and operations teams trust adoption, reliability and usage-charge reporting—and distinguish a release problem from a change in customer mix?

Method and analytical choices

Preserved the original two dashboards and audited the baseline before expanding. Separate fact grains, explicit KPI SQL, complete signup denominators and full observation windows support a third business workspace. An invented rate card is reconciled to closed-month invoices at the project-month grain.

Measured scenario evidence

  • Usage-charge expectation: 482.81 USD; invoice extract: 484.04 USD. One intentional 1.23 USD discrepancy remains visible.
  • Activation: 59/64. Ordered 30-day funnel: 64 → 62 → 59 → 33 through deployment.
  • Onboarding ITT lift: 6.68 pp; 95% interval −9.65 to 20.65 pp. Inconclusive.
  • The separate release investigation localizes a deliberately injected translation timeout regression and independently checks its arithmetic.

Recommendation and limits

Review the invoice exception before downstream reporting; investigate timeout configuration; collect stronger evidence before onboarding rollout. All organizations, prices, experiments and outcomes are invented. The original segment/region alignment is confounded. This demonstrates local dbt Core; dbt Cloud has been researched but has not been used.

02NYC 311: operational pressure without misleading averagesReal NYC public data · April–June 2025 arrival cohort+

Business question

How should an operations manager distinguish rising intake from slow closure when unresolved cases are still accumulating?

Method and analytical choices

Collected a bounded official API extract with ordered paging, count reconciliation and immutable hashes. A request-grain model retains open cases, flags invalid closure order and reconstructs backlog within the arrival cohort. Kaplan–Meier curves and mature seven-day rates complement completed-only medians. Rolling validation selects a forecast before a separate 14-day holdout.

Real analytical evidence

  • 195,941 requests; 182,307 valid closures and 13,634 unresolved at the historical cutoff.
  • 203 source records have closure before creation. Their requests stay in the denominator.
  • DEP’s June 24 peak: 3,721 arrivals, including 3,442 Water System requests.
  • DEP selected-model holdout MAE: 719.8 requests/day. The report preserves the poor accuracy.

Recommendation and limits

Add category-specific surge monitoring and contingency planning. Do not convert request forecasts into staffing savings without capacity data. The extract excludes earlier backlog and has no reopening event history. Different agencies have different mandates; these results do not rank productivity. 311 service requests are not contact-center call records, and the spike’s external cause is unassigned.

03GitHub: activity is a signal, not a customer metricReal public repositories · Calendar 2025, UTC+

Business question

What can a dependency-review or developer-relations team learn from public maintenance rhythms, issue closure and observed participation—and what remains unknown?

Method and analytical choices

Selected three complementary voice-software projects using explicit criteria. API pagination excludes PRs from issues and records rate/completeness evidence. Overlapping incremental captures, source-key updates, stale-version protection and fingerprinted database upserts make replay idempotent. A periodic full refresh reconciles deletions. Cohorts describe first observed linked human non-merge authors.

Real analytical evidence

  • 210 issues, 124 commit records, six releases across three public repositories.
  • 80 commits are non-merge; 24 distinct known human authors appear across the selected repositories.
  • The largest monthly increase is Silero VAD in August: 13 non-merge commits versus a prior-three-month mean of 0.67.
  • That burst’s known human non-merge activity is 13/13 from one linked author. The mechanism remains unproven.

Recommendation and limits

Review cadence, issue policies and maintainer context together before selecting a dependency. A concentrated burst is a question for review, not proof of staffing risk. Default-branch history, linked identities, deleted activity and left-truncated cohorts limit coverage. Public repository activity does not measure product usage, paying customers or employee productivity.
